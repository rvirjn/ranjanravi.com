1. Product structure

Keep current website features visible, add space for future growth

The app should not hide existing tools behind desktop-style tables. It should give one-tap access to current tools and reserve menu positions for upcoming modules without forcing another navigation redesign later.

01

Bottom navigation

Home, Kundali, Remedy, Calendar, and Menu remain reachable from every main screen.

02

Home as dashboard

Show free query usage, premium status, continue cards, and primary astrology actions.

03

Menu as all-options hub

Current pages, account, support, privacy, and future features live in a grouped menu.

04

Results become cards

Large website tables are converted into tabs, cards, sticky summary, and detail sheets.

Where each feature will live

7. Future menu and app expansion

Keep upcoming ideas discoverable without blocking the first release

The first app can ship with the current website functionality, while menu rows and empty states already reserve positions for future modules.

Saved Kundalis

Save birth profiles, edit details, and reopen reports from Home.

Reports

Export Kundali, remedies, divisional charts, and comparison summaries.

Panchang

Daily tithi, nakshatra, rahu kaal, and suggested actions on Home.

Horoscope

Daily, weekly, and monthly horoscope cards tied to saved birth profiles.

Kundali Matching

Use compare patterns for compatibility, family sharing, and score summaries.

Marriage

Marriage timing, muhurat guidance, and relationship-focused remedies.

Career

Career strengths, favorable periods, and work-related planetary guidance.

Child

Child-related guidance, auspicious birth planning, and family report sections.

Past Life

Dedicated exploratory report area for past-life themes and karmic indicators.

Pooja

Pooja suggestions, remedy rituals, mantras, and related support options.

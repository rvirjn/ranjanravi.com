Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Saptarishi (), operated by (“we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website, Android / iOS app, Kundali tools, accounts, and related astrology services.

By using Saptarishi, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Saptarishi, we may collect the following information.

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email, and a hashed password when you register or update your profile. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not in plain text.
  • Birth and astrology details you enter — name, date, time, and place of birth, plus latitude, longitude, and timezone derived from that place. We use this to generate kundali, comparison, auspicious timing, remedy, Nakshatra, Dasha, Yoga, Dosha, house, and divisional-chart results. For logged-in users we keep recent birth views so forms can be prefilled from Saved birth details.
  • Place for timings — city or place you select for auspicious timing searches, converted to coordinates. We do not access device GPS or request location permission.
  • Usage and device signals — public IP address, a guest ID stored in your browser, session tokens, query usage counts, and basic service or error logs to operate the service, keep it secure, and enforce free / premium limits. We do not use your public IP address to obtain your precise GPS location.
  • Premium, wallet, and coupon — plan tier, usage, expiry, coupon, wallet or entitlement status on your account. We do not collect card numbers; payments are completed via UPI / QR outside our card processing. Where a third-party payment method is used, that provider’s own privacy policy may also apply.
  • Site view counter — aggregated visit counts (not used to identify you personally).

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts, sessions, and authentication.
  • Generate kundali and related astrology calculations from the birth details you provide.
  • Convert a typed place name into geographic coordinates and timezone information.
  • Save birth profiles where you choose to use Saved birth details.
  • Apply free-tier and premium usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Manage purchases, premium access, wallet balances, coupons, and related entitlements.
  • Improve, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service.
  • Send transactional messages (for example registration or support) when email is configured.
  • Respond to support requests you send by email, WhatsApp, or phone.

3. Place lookup and geocoding

When you enter a place of birth or a place for timing calculations, we may send that place name to a geocoding service to obtain latitude, longitude, timezone, country, and administrative region. Our current configuration uses the Open-Meteo Geocoding API for this lookup.

Place lookup is used only to support location-dependent astronomical and astrology calculations. It is not your live device location. We do not send your password or account credentials as part of a place lookup request.

4. Swiss Ephemeris and astronomical calculations

Saptarishi uses the Swiss Ephemeris software library to calculate astronomical and planetary positions from the birth date, time, timezone, and coordinates you provide. That includes planetary longitudes, Ascendant / Lagna, houses, and lunar-node information used for Rahu and Ketu.

These calculations run as part of our application infrastructure. We do not share your account information with a separate third-party Kundali-generation service. Our own code then applies Vedic astrology rules to produce Rashi, Nakshatra, Pada, Dasha, Yogas, Doshas, house details, divisional charts, and other Kundali-related results.

A short-lived session flag may also use sessionStorage for site-view counting. Cookies or similar technologies may be used where needed for authentication, guest identification, usage tracking, or application preferences. Clearing browser storage may affect some features.

12. Children’s privacy

Saptarishi accounts are for people aged or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for children, and the app is not directed at children. You may enter another person’s birth details (for example a child’s kundali) to generate a chart. That information is stored on your account. Only do this if you are their parent or guardian or they have asked you to. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saptarishi (https://saptarishi.ranjanravi.com/), operated by ranjanravi.com (“we”, “us”), collects and uses information when you use our website and the Android / iOS app that loads the same service.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email, and a hashed password when you register or update your profile.
  • Birth details you enter — date, time, and place of birth, used to generate kundali, comparison, auspicious timing, and remedy results. For logged-in users we may keep recent birth views so forms can be prefilled.
  • Usage and device signals — public IP address, a guest ID stored in your browser, session tokens, and query usage counts to enforce free / premium limits and keep the service secure.
  • Premium / coupon — when you redeem a paid plan coupon, we store plan tier, usage, and expiry on your account. We do not collect card numbers; payments are completed via UPI / QR outside our card processing.
  • Site view counter — aggregated visit counts (not used to identify you personally).

2. How we use information

  • Provide kundali, auspicious timing, remedy, and related astrology tools.
  • Create and manage accounts, sessions, and premium access.
  • Apply free-tier limits and prevent abuse.
  • Send transactional messages (for example registration or support) when email is configured.
  • Respond to support requests you send by email, WhatsApp, or phone.

3. How we store and share information

  • Account and usage data are stored on our servers and related infrastructure (including our API at api.ranjanravi.com and database providers we use to operate the service).
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not in plain text.
  • Place lookup may use geocoding services to resolve birth place to coordinates.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage for login sessions (so you stay signed in across app restarts) and for a guest ID used in free-tier tracking. A short-lived session flag may also use sessionStorage for site-view counting.

5. Children’s privacy

Saptarishi is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it when required.

6. Data retention

We keep account, usage, and related records while your account is active and as needed to operate the service, enforce limits, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile details when logged in.
  • Request account or data deletion by email.
  • Stop using the service and uninstall the app at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures (including HTTPS and hashed passwords) to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

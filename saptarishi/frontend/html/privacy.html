Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Saptarishi (), operated by (“we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website, Android / iOS app, Kundali tools, accounts, and related astrology services.

By using Saptarishi, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Saptarishi, we may collect the following information.

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email, and a hashed password when you register or update your profile. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not in plain text.
  • Birth and astrology details you enter — name, date, time, and place of birth, plus latitude, longitude, and timezone derived from that place. We use this to generate kundali, comparison, auspicious timing, remedy, Nakshatra, Dasha, Yoga, Dosha, house, and divisional-chart results. If you tick Save while signed in, we keep those details as Saved birth details so forms can be prefilled later.
  • Place for timings — city or place you select for auspicious timing searches, converted to coordinates. We do not access device GPS or request location permission.
  • Usage and device signals — public IP address, a guest ID stored in your browser, session tokens, query usage counts, and basic service or error logs to operate the service, keep it secure, and enforce free / premium limits. We do not use your public IP address to obtain your precise GPS location.
  • Premium, wallet, and coupon — plan tier, usage, expiry, coupon, wallet or entitlement status on your account. We do not collect card numbers; payments are completed via UPI / QR outside our card processing. Where a third-party payment method is used, that provider’s own privacy policy may also apply.
  • Ask AI questions — if you are signed in and use Ask AI, the question text you type is sent to our servers and then to our AI inference provider (currently Groq) to generate an answer. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other secrets in Ask AI.
  • Site view counter — aggregated visit counts (not used to identify you personally).

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts, sessions, and authentication.
  • Generate kundali and related astrology calculations from the birth details you provide.
  • Convert a typed place name into geographic coordinates and timezone information.
  • Save birth profiles when you tick Save on a birth-details form.
  • Apply free-tier and premium usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Manage purchases, premium access, wallet balances, coupons, and related entitlements.
  • Improve, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service.
  • Send transactional messages (for example registration or support) when email is configured.
  • Answer Ask AI questions you submit while signed in.
  • Respond to support requests you send by email, WhatsApp, or phone.

3. Place lookup and geocoding

When you enter a place of birth or a place for timing calculations, we may send that place name to a geocoding service to obtain latitude, longitude, timezone, country, and administrative region. Our current configuration uses the Geoapify Geocoding API (Powered by Geoapify). Geoapify results are based on open geographic data, including OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Site-wide attribution is shown in the page footer as required by Geoapify’s free plan and by OpenStreetMap.

Place lookup is used only to support location-dependent astronomical and astrology calculations. It is not your live device location. We do not send your password, Ask AI questions, or account credentials as part of a place lookup request.

4. Astronomical calculations

Saptarishi calculates astronomical and planetary positions from the birth date, time, timezone, and coordinates you provide. That includes planetary longitudes, Ascendant / Lagna, houses, and lunar-node information used for Rahu and Ketu.

These calculations run on our servers using publicly available NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory planetary ephemerides (DE440s) through the Skyfield library (MIT License). NASA, JPL, and Caltech do not operate, sponsor, or endorse Saptarishi. We do not send your birth details to Swiss Ephemeris / Astrodienst, and we do not share your account information with a separate third-party Kundali-generation service. Our own code then applies Vedic astrology rules to produce Rashi, Nakshatra, Pada, Dasha, Yogas, Doshas, house details, divisional charts, and other Kundali-related results.

5. Ask AI

Ask AI is available to signed-in users. Your question is sent from the app to our API, then forwarded to Groq so a language model can generate a reply. Groq processes that prompt under Groq’s Services Agreement and related privacy terms. We do not send your password or payment details to Groq as part of Ask AI. Answers are general Vedic guidance and are not a substitute for a personal consultation.
